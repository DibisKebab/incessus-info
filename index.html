Help and contact

Incessus is an iPhone app for a research study on walking after brain surgery.

The study is run at the National Hospital for Neurology and Neurosurgery, Queen Square, London.

The app is only for people who are taking part in the study. You need a study code from the research team to use it. Without a code, the app does nothing.

What the app does

  1. You type your study code.
  2. You allow the app to read your Apple Health data.
  3. You press Send my data. The app sends your Health data, mainly how you walk, to the research team.

The app only reads. It never changes anything in Apple Health.

It sends nothing until you press Send my data.

The app does not give medical advice. If you are worried about your health, speak to your own doctor or nurse.

How to send your data

  1. Open Incessus and press Start.
  2. Type the study code the research team gave you. It looks like TH-0042. Then press Continue.
  3. Press Allow Health access. When your iPhone asks, choose Turn On All, then tap Allow. If it asks how much to share, choose All Recorded Data and Future Data.
  4. Press Send my data. Keep the app open and stay on Wi‑Fi. The first time takes several minutes.
  5. When you see All done, you can close the app.

Someone else can type the code or press the buttons for you.

If something does not work

The app says “That code is not on the study list.”
Please check the code with the research team. Please do not guess.
The app says “Could not connect.”
Check your Wi‑Fi, then try again.
The app says the research team’s computer is on, but its data store is locked.
Nothing was lost. Please tell the research team, then try again later.
The app says “This app cannot send yet.”
Nothing is wrong with your phone. Please contact the research team.
The app says “Nearly done.”
Your data was sent. Press the blue Send button again later to finish.
You pressed Stop, or the send stopped part of the way.
Nothing already sent is lost. Press Back to Send, then press the blue Send button. It carries on from where it stopped.
You want to change what the app may read.
On your iPhone, open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Health, then Incessus.

If you want to stop

Taking part is your choice. You can stop at any time. You do not have to give a reason.

To stop taking part, tell the research team.

You can also switch off the app’s Health access in Settings, or delete the app from your iPhone. The app then reads and sends nothing more.
